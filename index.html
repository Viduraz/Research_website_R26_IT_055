The project

Connected monitoring for assisted care

The platform brings together four research components to support awareness of who is present, where people move, whether a movement pattern appears unusual, and whether expected care activities take place.

Pose and gait features support privacy-conscious analysis. Events and activity records give caregivers a view of relevant changes over time.

  • Person tracking, safety zones and caregiver continuity
