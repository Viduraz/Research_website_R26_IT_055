AI that watches over elders, so caregivers can care.

The Secure Elder-Care Monitoring Platform uses camera-based skeleton and pose analysis to identify residents, follow their movement through safe zones, detect falls and unusual behaviour, and check that daily care routines actually happen. It does this with a strong focus on privacy.

Why this research matters

Elder care needs eyes that never tire and never intrude.

Falls, wandering, missed medication and unnoticed distress are among the biggest risks in assisted living. Caregivers cannot watch every room at every moment. Conventional CCTV records everything but understands nothing.

✓
Understands, not just recordsTurns raw video into meaningful care events.
✓
Privacy by designWorks from skeletal landmarks and movement, not faces.
✓
Built for caregiversSurfaces only the events that need attention.
Our research goal

One connected platform that knows who is present, where they are, how they are moving, and whether their care is on schedule.

  • Identify residents even when faces are hidden
  • Keep people safe inside configurable zones
  • Spot falls, aggression and prolonged inactivity
  • Verify that daily routines take place

How it works

From camera frame to caregiver alert

A single pipeline connects all four components, so every event carries context about who, where and what.

Capture

Camera frames stream in from monitored rooms.

Extract pose

Skeletal landmarks replace raw pixels.

Identify & track

Know who each person is and where they move.

Analyse

Detect anomalies, zone events and activities.

Alert

Caregivers receive timely, relevant notifications.
