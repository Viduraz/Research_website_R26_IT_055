Tracking, Geofencing & Caregiver Continuity

D.M.D.L. Dedunu’s component tracks people across video frames, monitors configured safety zones and checks caregiver visibility.

D.M.D.L. Dedunu · Team Member

Component overview · spatial safety

Follow people and monitor defined zones

The service detects people in camera frames, maintains track identities across temporary occlusion, records short movement trajectories and checks whether a person enters a configured safe, restricted or alert zone.

It also estimates zone exits from recent trajectory and bounding-box history, and tracks whether an assigned caregiver remains visible.

Component pipeline

This page describes the implementation details supplied for this component.

Tracking and spatial checks

Tracks remain useful through brief interruptions

01

Person tracking

YOLOv8 Nano person detections feed ByteTrack or BoT-SORT. The documented fallback uses IoU matching and centroid distance when a tracker ID is lost. Recent tracks remain active briefly and stale tracks are purged.

Documented settings: detection confidence >0.30; active tracking 1.5 seconds; stale-track purge 3 seconds; fallback IoU threshold 0.15.

02

Depth-aware geofences

Administrators define polygonal safe, restricted and alert zones. The service checks a person’s foot point against a zone and uses estimated camera distance and posture to reduce false breach alerts. Polygons require at least three vertices.

03

Room-exit alerts

A recent maximum person-box area is compared with the last observed area if someone disappears from the view. The configured shrink and disappearance criteria are used to infer a possible exit and raise an alert.

Caregiver continuity

Escalate extended absence for review

The component checks caregiver visibility in the camera view and raises progressively stronger states at the configured intervals.

10 sec
Warning state when a caregiver is out of frame.
30 sec
Missing state with notify severity.
120 sec
Critical missing state after two minutes.

Recorded data

Spatial and presence history

The supplied component details list tracking_logs, geofence_zones, geofence_alerts, verified_caregiver_sessions, tracking_alerts and face_logs.

This component is documented as FastAPI on port 8002, with a React/Vite dashboard and asynchronous identity requests to the face-verification service.
