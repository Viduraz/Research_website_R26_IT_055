Schedule & Activity Monitoring

Chamathka B.M.G.N.’s component observes activities, matches them against care routines and records completion, lateness and missed tasks.

Chamathka B.M.G.N. · Team Member
Student ID: IT22143686 · Component Owner
Component overview · daily care routines

Compare observed activity with expected routines

The browser-based monitor uses webcam frames and pose landmarks to estimate selected daily activities. Activity observations are compared with a resident’s schedule, and the system records status changes for caregiver review.

The current implementation notes identify pose geometry and temporal rules as the active recognition path. Optional Random Forest and LSTM code paths are present, but their feature flags are off.

Component pipeline

This page describes the implementation details supplied for this component.

Activity recognition

Combine posture, motion and context

The active browser pipeline smooths short-term predictions and uses multiple visual cues for activity decisions.

01

Movement and posture

BlazePose landmarks support posture geometry, body velocity, leg angles and short-term temporal movement checks.

02

Object context

COCO-SSD detections provide optional scene context for activities such as eating or drinking; face landmarks are also part of the documented browser pipeline.

03

Supported activities

The current active classifier contract covers walking, sitting/resting, sleeping, eating and drinking.

Routine decisions

Activity events become schedule statuses

Early or completed

An observed activity is matched against its configured routine window and recorded as early or completed according to the active timing logic.

Late or missed

A late activity remains distinguishable from a missed task. The monitoring service evaluates missed activities after the permitted window.

Caregiver missing

If a routine requires caregiver presence and the condition is not satisfied, the activity can be recorded as caregiver-missing / not done.

Demonstration timing: the supplied schedule service notes describe accelerated demo schedules, with the first activity five minutes after schedule creation and subsequent activities one minute apart.

Monitoring and reporting

Notifications and records

Give caregivers a history to review

Activity logs, notification records, deviations and daily archives support dashboards, missed-task review and daily/weekly reporting. The supplied notes identify MongoDB collections such as activity_logs, notifications, deviations and daily_archives.

The source notes mention a fixed patient ID in schedule routes; this site does not claim a fully active multi-user authorization flow for those routes.
